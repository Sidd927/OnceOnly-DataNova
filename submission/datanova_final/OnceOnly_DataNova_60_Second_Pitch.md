# OnceOnly: 60-Second Pitch

About 150 words, roughly 60 seconds spoken. Every figure is in the claim-evidence audit.

---

A carbon registry can prove a credit was retired. It can't tell you how many public climate claims are relying on those same retired units.

Take a real Verra retirement from August 2026: 19,999 units, no beneficiary named. Two companies in our demo each claim part of it. Each claim matches the registry on its own, and even the totals add up. But 4,559 units are claimed by both.

OnceOnly catches this because it treats every serial number as an exact range of units inside its registry batch. It doesn't stop at "overlap". It works out whether it's a legitimate split, a broker acting for a client, related companies, an unnamed buyer, or a real conflict. Then it explains every signal with its evidence and its limits.

A smart contract enforces one rule: no two active claims can hold the same units.

Every retired tonne, claimed once.
