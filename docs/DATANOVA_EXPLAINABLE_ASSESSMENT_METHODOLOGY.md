### 1. Investigative Posture & Ethical Principles

The voluntary carbon market operates across decentralized registries, diverse national jurisdictions, varying commercial disclosure standards, and evolving intermediary broker structures. In this environment, computational analytical systems must never adopt the role of automated prosecutorial engines or unexplainable "fraud detectors."

#### Core Axioms of Explainable Assessment:
1. **Analytical Signal $\neq$ Legal Culpability:** An anomalous analytical signal (such as extreme beneficiary concentration, rapid temporal velocity, or cross-registry identifier similarity) indicates a phenomenon requiring human audit—never criminal intent, market manipulation, or intentional double claiming.
2. **Strict Non-Accusatory Terminology:** The OnceOnly platform systematically enforces non-accusatory language. Evaluated targets are classified into neutral evidentiary tiers:
   - `NO_MATERIAL_SIGNAL`
   - `REVIEW_RECOMMENDED`
   - `ELEVATED_REVIEW_PRIORITY`
   - `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`
   - `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
   Labels such as "fraudulent," "suspicious," "bad actor," or "violator" are strictly prohibited in code, API responses, and documentation.
3. **Rejection of Opaque Scoring:** Aggregated probabilistic scoring (e.g., "Risk: 87/100") hides critical assumptions, conceals conflicting signals, creates an illusion of quantitative certainty, and exposes auditors to severe confirmation bias. OnceOnly produces structured **Evidence Inventories** with discrete rationale, supporting records, mathematical thresholds, provenance, limitations, counterevidence, and missing information.

#### A. Interval Allocation & Reconciliation (Phase 2B)
Retirement claims represent half-open or closed integer serial ranges $[s, e] \subset \mathbb{Z}^+$. For candidate claim $[s_c, e_c]$ and existing ledger claim $[s_a, e_a]$ within namespace $N = \text{registry}:\text{batch}$:
$$\text{Overlap Interval } I = [s_c, e_c] \cap [s_a, e_a] = [\max(s_c, s_a), \min(e_c, e_a)]$$
$$\text{Overlap Quantity } Q_I = \max(0, \min(e_c, e_a) - \max(s_c, s_a) + 1)$$
- If $Q_I = 0$: Strict disjointness. If both claims belong to the same parent batch and legal entity, this represents a `LEGITIMATE_SPLIT` (`RULE-REC-004`).
- If $Q_I = e_c - s_c + 1 = e_a - s_a + 1$: Exact identity (`RULE-REC-001`).
- If $0 < Q_I < \max(e_c - s_c + 1, e_a - s_a + 1)$: Partial intersection (`RULE-REC-002`) or containment (`RULE-REC-003`).

#### B. Beneficiary Concentration (Phase 2C Stage 1)
Given a batch of $N$ disclosed retirement events with quantities $q_1, q_2, \dots, q_k$ across $K$ unique entities:
$$\text{Market Share } s_i = \frac{q_i}{\sum_{j=1}^K q_j}, \quad \text{HHI} = \sum_{i=1}^K (100 \cdot s_i)^2 \in [0, 10000]$$
- $\text{HHI} \ge 8,000$ with $s_1 \ge 0.90$ triggers `RULE-CON-001` (Single-Beneficiary Dominance).
- $\text{HHI} \ge 2,500$ across $K \ge 3$ triggers `RULE-CON-002` (High Concentration).
- Undisclosed Volume Share:
  $$U_{\text{share}} = \frac{Q_{\text{undisclosed}}}{Q_{\text{total}}}$$
  $U_{\text{share}} \ge 0.50$ triggers `RULE-CON-003` (Material Undisclosed Share).

#### C. Batch Fragmentation & Micro-Claims (Phase 2C Stage 1)
Event size distribution inequality is measured via the Gini coefficient:
$$G = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n |q_i - q_j|}{2n \sum_{i=1}^n q_i}$$
$G \ge 0.70$ triggers `RULE-FRG-001` (Extreme Fragmentation).
Micro-claim density evaluates retail aggregation:
$$D_{\text{micro}} = \frac{|\{i \mid q_i < 10.0\}|}{n}, \quad V_{\text{micro}} = \frac{\sum_{i \in \text{micro}} q_i}{\sum q_i}$$
Triggering `RULE-FRG-002` when $|\{i \mid q_i < 10\}| \ge 20$ and $V_{\text{micro}} \ge 0.30$.

#### D. Nonparametric Temporal Dynamics (Phase 2C Stage 2)
Retirement velocity series $x_t$ are evaluated using robust Median Absolute Deviation (MAD), eliminating Gaussian distribution assumptions in heavy-tailed market data:
$$\text{Median } \tilde{x} = \text{median}(x), \quad \text{MAD} = \text{median}(|x_t - \tilde{x}|)$$
$$\text{Modified } Z_t = \frac{0.6745 \cdot (x_t - \tilde{x})}{\text{MAD}}$$
$Z_t \ge 3.0$ and $x_t \ge 100\text{ tonnes}$ triggers `RULE-TMP-001` (Statistically Anomalous Burst).

#### E. Cross-Registry Fuzzy Project Similarity (Phase 2C Stage 3)
Project title strings across Verra VCS and Gold Standard are evaluated via token set and sequence matching:
$$S_{\text{weighted}} = 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{set\_ratio}} + 0.30 \cdot S_{\text{token\_sort}} + 0.30 \cdot S_{\text{levenshtein\_ratio}}$$
- $S_{\text{weighted}} \ge 0.85 \land \text{Country}_A = \text{Country}_B \implies \text{RULE-XREG-001}$.
- $S_{\text{weighted}} \ge 0.70 \land \text{Country}_A = \text{Country}_B \implies \text{RULE-XREG-002}$.
- $\text{ID}_A = \text{ID}_B \land (\text{Country}_A \ne \text{Country}_B \lor S_{\text{weighted}} < 0.50) \implies \text{RULE-XREG-003}$ (Identifier Namespace Collision).

### 3. Double-Counting Safeguards & Correlation Grouping Proof

#### The Problem of Collinear Signal Inflation
In traditional heuristic engines, if a single batch exhibits:
1. Top beneficiary share = 95%
2. HHI = 9,100
3. Undisclosed volume share = 55%

An additive or weighted scoring system would sum their scores:
$$\text{Score} = w_{\text{top}} + w_{\text{HHI}} + w_{\text{undisclosed}} \implies \text{CRITICAL / URGENT}$$

This is methodologically fraudulent: **all three metrics are mathematical projections of the exact same underlying `beneficiary` column.** High undisclosed share reduces the disclosed denominator, mechanically driving HHI upwards. Treating them as three independent corroborating pieces of evidence artificially manufactures an illusion of compound malfeasance.

#### The Dependency Grouping Proof
Let $\mathcal{E} = \{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_m\}$ be the set of triggered evidence items.
We define an equivalence relation $\sim_D$ over $\mathcal{E}$ based on shared data origin:
$$e_j \sim_D e_k \iff \text{DataSource}(e_j) = \text{DataSource}(e_k) \land \text{TargetEntity}(e_j) = \text{TargetEntity}(e_k)$$

The partition of $\mathcal{E}$ under $\sim_D$ yields disjoint dependency groups:
$$\mathcal{E} / \sim_D = \{G_1, G_2, \dots, G_p\}$$

Each group $G_i$ is mapped to a single `EvidenceDependencyGroup`:
- **Primary Signal:** The item $e^* \in G_i$ with the highest severity $\max(\text{Severity}(e))$.
- **Correlated Reinforcements:** All remaining items $e \in G_i \setminus \{e^*\}$ are designated with:
  $$\text{is\_reinforcing\_not\_independent} = \text{True}$$
- **Independent Evidence Count:**
  $$C_{\text{independent}} = |\mathcal{E} / \sim_D|$$
- **Priority Invariant:** Review priority is determined solely across the set of independent primary signals:
  $$\text{Priority}(\mathcal{E}) = \max_{G_i \in \mathcal{E}/\sim_D} \left( \text{Priority}(\text{Primary}(G_i)) \right)$$
  **Correlated secondary signals cannot escalate the priority.** A cluster of moderate signals sharing the same dependency group remains strictly `ReviewPriority.MEDIUM`.

### 4. Counterevidence Rules & Balanced Adjudication

An assessment that catalogs only anomalies is inherently biased towards false positives. The OnceOnly framework mandates that every evidence collection pipeline explicitly search for and document **counterevidence**.

To maintain audit integrity, OnceOnly formally categorizes counterevidence into two distinct classes:
1. **Computed / Observed Counterevidence:** Mathematical and ledger facts derived directly from dataset census calculations or point-set algebra (e.g., zero unit intersection $Q_I = 0$, distinct national jurisdictions $\text{Country}_A \ne \text{Country}_B$, or exact disclosed unit counts).
2. **Domain-Contextual Hypotheses & Commercial Caveats:** Market operational principles embedded in rule definitions to guide human auditors (e.g., anchor buyer practices, retail micro-offset aggregators). These remind investigators that statistical concentration or micro-transactions often stem from legitimate commercial structures rather than illicit behavior, preventing premature accusations without claiming private off-chain contracts were inspected.

| Anomaly Signal | Counterevidence Type | Required Counterevidence Check & Grounding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `RULE-CON-001` (Single-Beneficiary Dominance) | Contextual Caveat + Observed Metric | Contextual: Anchor buyer agreements are standard corporate practice. Observed: Documents total disclosed units ($Q_{\text{disclosed}}$) and unique claimants count ($K$). |
| `RULE-FRG-001` (Micro-Claim Fragmentation) | Contextual Caveat | Contextual: Retail carbon offset programs (e-commerce cart checkouts, travel offsets) legitimately produce high micro-claim volumes. |
| `RULE-TMP-001` (Temporal Burst) | Contextual Caveat + Observed Metric | Contextual: Corporate fiscal compliance deadlines create seasonal bursts. Observed: Evaluates baseline lookback median and dispersion. |
| `RULE-REC-004` (Disjoint Split Allocation) | Computed / Observed Fact | Observed / Mathematical: Algorithmic interval verification confirms zero shared serial units ($Q_I = 0$). |
| `RULE-XREG-002` (Identifier Collision) | Computed / Observed Fact | Observed: Confirms independent, disjoint registry namespace counters rather than project identity. |
| `RULE-XREG-003` (Contradictory Attributes) | Computed / Observed Fact | Observed: National jurisdiction contradiction ($\text{Country}_A \ne \text{Country}_B$) proves distinct physical assets. |

### 5. Missing Data Policy: Non-Imputation and Transparency

In voluntary carbon accounting, missing information is ubiquitous:
- Many corporate buyers utilize commercial non-disclosure provisions allowed by registry terms.
- Wholesale brokers frequently hold credits in omnibus retirement accounts before client distribution.
- Legacy registry records often omit vintage end dates or precise project coordinates.

#### OnceOnly Non-Imputation Principles:
1. **Never Impute Beneficiary Identity:** OnceOnly never imputes, guesses, or assigns surrogate identities to missing beneficiaries. If a record states "Not Disclosed" or is empty, it is tracked explicitly under `unresolved_quantity` and evaluated under Dual-Track Disclosure Accounting.
2. **Missing Information Itemization:** Every assessment response features a top-level `missing_information_items` array. The absence of data is reported as an analytical limitation, not an accusation of obfuscation.
3. **Threshold for Insufficient Evidence:** If key identifiers required for a scope (e.g., missing entity names, unparseable serial units) are absent, the engine immediately returns `AssessmentStatus.INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` rather than calculating speculative metrics.
